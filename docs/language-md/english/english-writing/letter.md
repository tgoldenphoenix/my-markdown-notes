## Thanks your teacher

Hi, Matt! I want to take a moment to share something personal and tell you how deeply grateful I am for the work you’ve done through the College Prep School channel.

I had never read a math textbook in my life. I took every single one of your courses from Arithmetic, Pre-Algebra, Beginning Algebra, Geometry, Intermediate Algebra, Trigonometry, Precalculus, and up to Calculus 1. Whenever I had questions, I would leave them under the videos on YouTube, and you always answered. Those courses have been extremely important for my math education. They didn’t just teach me content; they gave me a real foundation.

When I was almost at the last video of Calculus 1, I started wondering how I was going to continue learning. I knew I needed to review everything I had learned from Arithmetic all the way up to Calculus. It had been quite the journey. I began searching for other channels so I could go through the material again with a different source. The more I searched, the more I started to panic. I couldn’t find any channel with videos that matched the quality of what you built, with real classes, homework assignments, test prep, tests, and exams. Nothing came close.

Thankfully, by that point I had become mature enough to try reading textbooks. I asked for book recommendations, and you guided me to the OpenStax books from Rice University. I downloaded every single math textbook they offer, twelve books in total, ranging from the lower levels all the way up to Calculus 3. That’s about 15,000 pages. I decided I wanted to read all of them. I divided them into levels so I could review the foundations, stay connected to intermediate material, and keep myself in touch with Calculus at the same time. It’s been two weeks since I started that journey.

The more I read, the more clearly I realize that the education I received from your courses was high quality. I simply would not have been able to open those dense textbooks and make sense of them without the foundation you gave me. Math textbooks can feel intimidating, but because of the way you taught, I had the right preparation. And I’m truly enjoying the journey.

One of the things that first triggered my interest in mathematics is that I love languages. For most of my life I was told that math is the study of numbers, shapes, structure, and change. No one ever told me that mathematics also has the properties of a language, almost like a kind of sign language, in a rough sense. That realization is what led me into this whole path: learning a new language as an adult. The language through which the laws of the universe were written, as Isaac Newton put it.

As I progressed through your courses, I kept hearing you talk about the language of mathematics, about the need to gain fluency in algebra in order to succeed in calculus, about learning mathematical notation properly so you can move forward. Little by little, the idea that had drawn me in was being confirmed by the way you taught. Most people like mathematics (or any subject) for different reasons. Mine was reinforced again and again on your channel by the consistent message that mathematics is a language. And now, as I read the OpenStax textbooks you recommended, I keep finding the same ideas. It feels like everything is connecting.

I’m so grateful for your work and for everything you have done. Your courses have been a life saver for me. They gave me access to a rigorous, structured path when I needed it most, and they prepared me to keep going independently.

If anyone reading this is struggling with mathematics or feels stuck, I want you to know that a solid foundation changes everything. The right teaching can take you from never having opened a textbook to being able to sit with thousands of pages of dense material and actually understand and enjoy it. Fluency takes time, but it is possible. The language of mathematics can be learned, even as an adult, and it is worth every hour you put into it. It's been 7 years since you started posting these videos. 7 years of hard-work to bless the world with your knowledge. Thank you so very, Matt. Truly. God bless you.
